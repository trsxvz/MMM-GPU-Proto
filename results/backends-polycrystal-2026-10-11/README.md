# GPU backends on the polycrystal (expensive behaviour, 11/10/2026)

Script: `cases/measure-backends-polycrystal.sh`. Polycrystal of
mm-opera-hpc (5 grains, single crystal behaviour of UO2, orthotropic,
finite strain, implicit), 10 steps, one process and the GPU, everything by
kernels. Behaviour integrated on the host (`generic` interface, reference)
or on the GPU with the library generated by the `generic-parallel`
interface for each backend that works without modifying the program
(`build/cases-others/polycrystal`). Executable of mm-opera-hpc from
`build/mm-opera-hpc`, built on the evening of 10/10 and statically linked
to mfem-mgis (de2e76a913 or earlier; the "mfem-mgis" line of
`summary.txt` gives the version of the sources). Quiet machine, two
passes.

Macroscopic stresses: relative gap of at most 2.4e-13 with respect to the
first computation with the `generic` behaviour (70 values). Same Newton
iterations (98) in the ten computations.

| Library | Total | Integration (158 calls) | Solve |
|---|---|---|---|
| `generic` (host) | 109.73 and 108.67 | 102.18 and 101.19 | 5.53 and 5.52 |
| cuda (nvcc) | 12.81 and 12.83 | 5.57 and 5.58 | 5.53 and 5.54 |
| hip (hipcc, NVIDIA platform) | 12.86 and 12.84 | 5.58 and 5.58 | 5.57 and 5.56 |
| cuda (clang) | 14.92 and 14.87 | 7.67 and 7.65 | 5.56 and 5.51 |
| hip (clang, NVIDIA platform) | 14.90 and 14.98 | 7.67 and 7.68 | 5.54 and 5.58 |

- On the GPU, the integration is 18 times faster (13 times with clang)
  than on one core of the host (one process, `generic` behaviour).
- The two toolchains that go through nvcc (cuda by nvcc, hip by hipcc,
  which calls nvcc on the NVIDIA platform) give the same time. The two
  that go through clang (cuda and hip) take 38 % more time on the
  integration (nvcc 27 % less than clang), and 16 % end to end. The
  language of the source (cuda or hip) does not matter, the compiler does.
- kokkos is not measured here: the program of mm-opera-hpc does not
  initialise Kokkos (see `results/backends-gpu-2026-10-11`). It is
  measured in `results/kokkos-polycrystal-2026-10-11`, with mfem-mgis
  built with Kokkos (option `enable-kokkos`), which initialises it.
