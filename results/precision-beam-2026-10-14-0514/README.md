# Float against double precision on the CPU, beam (14/10/2026)

Beam r4 of the prototype (111,843 unknowns), 10 steps, Newton, PCG of hypre
preconditioned by BoomerAMG (tolerance 1e-4), von Mises behaviour with
linear hardening. Two processes, one per physical core (cores 0 and 1,
with their twins 8 and 9), quiet machine (the neighbouring session does
not compile, the compilation of MFEM in float for the GPU is suspended).
Three repetitions, with alternating precisions. Script:
`cases/measure-precision-beam.sh`, summary: `cases/summary-precision.py`,
time per phase: `cases/summary-profiling.py`.

The two builds only differ by the precision: twin spack environments
`deps/spack/mmm-double-cpu` and `deps/spack/mmm-float-cpu` (MFEM 4.10,
hypre 3.2.0 and MUMPS in double or in single precision, without CUDA),
MGIS in double or in float, same mfem-mgis (gpu-proto 283e0166e9),
behaviour generated by mfront in the type of the build.

| Computation | Newton criterion | Min. time (s) | Median (s) | Iterations | Norm of the displacements |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| double | 1e-8 (the one of the case) | 34.27 | 34.37 | 28 | 48.83704586 |
| double | 1e-4 | 23.12 | 23.40 | 16 | 48.83715795 |
| float | 1e-4 | 20.74 | 20.83 | 16 | 48.83312988 |

Time per phase of the first run at 1e-4 (seconds, number of calls):

| Computation | Total | Integration | Residual | Tangent | Solution | Prediction |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| double | 23.49 | 0.76 (26) | 0.49 (26) | 3.53 (16) | 11.33 (16) | 7.25 (10) |
| float | 20.93 | 0.69 (26) | 0.48 (26) | 3.42 (16) | 9.52 (16) | 6.71 (10) |

- With the same criterion, float is 1.11 times faster, with the same Newton
  iterations. The gain comes mostly from the linear solution (1.19 times)
  and from the prediction (1.08 times); the integration of the behaviours,
  the residual and the tangent almost do not change (element by element
  computations without vectorisation that would benefit from single
  precision).
- The norm of the displacements in float differs by 8.0e-5 from that of
  double converged at 1e-8 (against 2.3e-6 for double at 1e-4): the gap
  comes from the precision of the computations, accumulated over the ten
  plasticity steps.
- The criterion of the case (1e-8), bounded to 100 ε = 1.2e-5 by mfem-mgis
  in float, is not reached at r4: the relative residual levels off around
  1.3e-5 and Newton stops after 50 iterations (first run,
  `results/precision-beam-2026-10-14-0506`). At r2 (2,475 unknowns), the
  floor was below the bound. The floor grows with the size of the problem:
  in float, the Newton tolerance must be chosen above it (1e-4 here).
