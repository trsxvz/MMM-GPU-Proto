# Analyses of the polycrystal law kernel (2026-11-22, series 3)

Polycrystal law (Mono_UO2_Cosh_Jaco3), generic-parallel interface, data
component by component on GPU, RTX 5080 for the times; H100 (`sm_90`) and
MI300A (`gfx942`) static numbers only. Working directories under
`scratch/night-2026-11-22/` (agent-lu-amd, agent-reconv, agent-localmem).

## Scratch frame of MI300A and thelfer/tfel#1501

- With #1501 on series 2, the scratch frame of the law kernel on MI300A grew
  from 6424 to 7848 B per lane in float and from 8936 to 11504 B in double.
- Cause: the AMDGPU limit on the number of basic blocks when inlining
  (`-amdgpu-inline-max-bb`, 1100, `GCNTTIImpl::areInlineCompatible`; the
  remark says "conflicting target features"). The tangent operator of the law
  was inlined at 12 call sites. #1501 lets clang unroll the 6x6 LU with a
  local permutation (44 -> 68 basic blocks), so 2 of the 12 copies were no
  longer inlined and their frames were stacked.
- With T1 (series 3), the tangent operator is instantiated once: 6040 B with
  #1501, 6048 B without in float; 8672 B and 8728 B in double. #1501 is kept
  as is. An alternative form keeping the gain without the growth exists
  (local branch `lu-decomposition-loops-amd`, `0e02783ff`), not needed.
- The size of the frame does not change the occupancy (128 VGPRs, 4 waves per
  SIMD). ROCr allocates `private_segment_size x 64 x slots x CUs` of scratch
  per queue: about 3.0 GB in float and 4.2 GB in double for the whole MI300A
  with series 3.
- CPU comparisons of two builds of the law below about 5 to 9 % are code
  placement on the Zen 5 of the development machine: the 6x6 LU runs in 40
  or 67 ns depending on a 16-byte shift; `-falign-functions=64` moves the
  reference by 9 %. Instruction counts (callgrind, x86-64-v3 builds) are used
  instead.

## Threads of a warp after the Newton loop

- The threads of a warp leaving the Newton loop at different iterations do
  not reconverge after it: the convergence exit of
  `TinyNonLinearSolverBase::solveNonLinearSystem2` jumps directly into the
  rest of `integrate` (`BREAK.RELIABLE`), and the failure exits bypass it,
  so the only meeting point is after `integrate`. Everything after the loop,
  tangent operator included, runs about twice per warp (28.7 % of the
  instructions executed per warp in double).
- Pure control flow rewrites are undone by NVVM (identical SASS). A
  convergence flag declared `volatile` in device code only
  (`TFEL_DEVICE_CODE`) gives the join (local branch `newton-reconvergence`,
  `c6ebdb0f3`, not in the series). It costs 4 instructions per warp and
  leaves the host code identical to the byte.
- Double: -15.2 % (12 batch sizes from 100000 to 1200000 points: -14.8 to
  -15.6 %), outputs identical to the bit.
- Float: irregular with the batch size, from -15 % to +33 %, only when the
  tangent operator is computed; bimodal and stable per binary and size,
  mechanism not identified. Not integrated.

## Local memory traffic of the float kernel

Per point (n = 1000000, DPK1_DF): 4675 B loaded, 6622 B stored, 3.49
evaluations of the residual.

| Source | loaded / stored (B per point) | stall samples |
|---|---|---|
| Integrator of the law (`computeFdF`) | 1157 / 3026 | 40 % |
| LU and substitutions of the Newton algorithm | 1228 / 432 | 9 % |
| Construction of the behaviour | 0 / 940 | 2 % |
| Tangent operator: LU, substitutions, code of the law | 1008 / 788 | 26 % |
| Conversion DTAU_DDF -> DPK1_DF | 314 / 326 | 4 % |
| Runtime: tangent copied to the local array, scatter, gather, pointers | 886 / 840 | 12 % |

- Avoidable, by bytes per point: final values of the law written at each
  evaluation (777 B, law); dead initialisations in `@Integrator` (766 B,
  law); arrays read with a dynamic index (670 B loaded, law); dead
  initialisation of the jacobian and of the residual in `computeFdF` (586 B,
  generator); zero fill of the tensorial local variables (408 B, not a
  default: a law reading them before assigning them would change); copies
  of the tangent operator (runtime, generator).
- Prototypes (patches of the generated header): with the tangent operator,
  27 % fewer stores give -4.5 % to +1.9 % depending on the batch size: the
  dirty part of the stack goes to DRAM about once per point whatever the
  repeated stores. Without the tangent operator, the same cut gives -15 to
  -18 %, regularly. The dead initialisation of `computeFdF` alone is
  bit-identical everywhere, about -2 % with the tangent operator and -5 to
  -6 % without.
- With fewer stores, the reconvergence above has 2 slow batch sizes of 15
  instead of 8: the traffic of the tangent phase must come down first.
