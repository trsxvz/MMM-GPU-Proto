# Journal

This page is a short timeline of the main milestones of the prototype.
The full log, day by day, with the defects found, the decisions and
their reasons, is `JOURNAL.md` (in French) in the repository. Each entry
of the log describes the state at the time it was written; the current
state is described in `docs/`.

All times are those of the development machine, with an NVIDIA RTX 5080
GPU.

## 04/10/2026: preliminary study

- A study outside this repository compared, on a J2 plasticity beam, a
  fixed point on the elastic operator K0 accelerated by Anderson with
  Newton: faster on the beam, slower on a polycrystal. It motivated the
  solver work on mfem-mgis.

## 05/10/2026: TFEL, the `generic-parallel` interface

- Survey of TFEL master: the `generic-parallel` interface already existed
  for material properties; the generated code of behaviours was almost
  GPU compatible.
- Milestone 1: code generated by MFront compilable for the GPU.
- Milestone 2: the `generic-parallel` interface for behaviours, with the
  logic of one point written once in headers.
- Milestone 3: comparison tests against the `generic` interface, and a
  compilation sweep of the test behaviours.
- Backends: CUDA, HIP on NVIDIA GPUs, C++ parallel algorithms (also on
  the GPU with nvc++), Kokkos, SYCL, OpenMP. First nvc++ and nvcc
  defects found, with reproducers.
- Coverage (B1 to B7): finite strain, initialize functions,
  post-processings, rotations of orthotropic behaviours, single
  crystals, standalone models, the TDLS linear solver.

## 06/10/2026: compilers and portability

- Defects of OpenMP offloading in nvc++ isolated; reproducers replayed
  with newer NVHPC versions.
- Material properties and external variables given variable by
  variable.
- clang CUDA and HIP configurations; sweep of all MFront tests with
  clang CUDA.
- Matrices of tests now run at `-O3` only.
- Corrections of the first review (R1), and portable test behaviours
  (R2): `tfel::fail`, `abortResolution`, `abortIntegration`,
  `@SharedData`.

## 07/10/2026: validation

- All test behaviours of MFront compared at run time, not only compiled
  (R3); several defects of TFEL found and corrected.
- Validations at `-O3` (R4), then switch to the spack environment
  `mmm-gpu`, validated: 15 configurations of behaviours (kokkos and
  kokkos-host counted apart, 14 otherwise) and 16 of material
  properties.

## 08/10/2026: TFEL finished, MGIS batches

- clang-tidy and independent reviews (R5); shared data copied per GPU,
  symbols and less duplication (R6); parameters of behaviour variables
  and rotation of all tangent blocks (R7).
- Errors at no cost on success (R8): a synchronous CUDA call on 1000
  points goes from 135 to 29 µs.
- MGIS: loading and integration of batches, memory location and memory
  resources, material properties and models, asynchronous computation.
- TFEL: memory space symbol (R9), execution options and asynchronous
  evaluation of material properties (R10), documentation (R11),
  statistics of material properties modelled on behaviours (R12), final
  audit of TFEL and MGIS (R13).
- Choices for mfem-mgis decided (D2).

## 09/10/2026: MGIS views, MFEM 4.10, mfem-mgis L0 to L6

- MGIS: views and layouts (component by component, arrays owned by the
  caller), end of step on the GPU; then MGIS locked.
- Detailed plan of mfem-mgis in work packages L0 to L10, reviewed
  against the whole code.
- The whole prototype moves to MFEM 4.10, built with CUDA and all the
  solver libraries.
- L0: the three repositories build and pass their tests against MFEM
  4.10; reference times.
- L1: data at integration points in the memory of MFEM.
- L2: behaviours integrated by batches on the CPU; on the polycrystal,
  the behaviour goes from 104.9 s to 17.8 s with `openmp` and 13.9 s
  with `stlpar`.
- L3: gradients, internal forces and tangent by kernels, direct
  assembly into the sparse matrix.
- First complete computations on the GPU, behaviour integrated on the
  GPU, prediction by kernels and end of step on the GPU: beam r4 in
  3.3 s on the GPU against 17.5 s on 8 cores; polycrystal in 13.2 s
  against 31.7 s.

## 10/10/2026: solvers and the whole suite on the GPU

- Matrix free tangent action and preconditioner on the assembled
  tangent.
- `FixedPoint` solver with all accelerations of MTest, BoomerAMG
  options, `NewtonK0`, inexact Newton: on the GPU, the beam takes 1.96 s
  with NewtonK0 and Eisenstat-Walker against 3.12 s with Newton.
- Thermal problems and micromorphic damage by kernels; Jacobians
  computed by a kernel.
- The whole test suite of mfem-mgis passes with all computations forced
  to kernels, and on the GPU (except the two tests that require the
  absence of a GPU).
- AmgX wrapper in mfem-mgis; pull requests opened to MFEM (#5604) and
  AmgX (#386).

## 11/10/2026: kernels everywhere

- FBar and Faltus regularisation by kernels; boundary conditions
  independent of the unknowns; other integrators of the form.
- `IntegrationRuleOrder`, positions of the matrix entries computed once,
  products without forming B, inverse Jacobians stored, global matrix
  with kept pattern and exchanges.
- Beam scaling: the GPU is 10.9 times faster than 8 cores at 840,000
  unknowns. Option for the size of the hypre memory pool.
- ex8 of mfem-mgis-examples entirely on the GPU.
- Behaviours of the CUDA, HIP and Kokkos backends used by mfem-mgis;
  optional Kokkos initialisation.
- Cermet and bubble of mm-opera-hpc on the batch path; standard host
  memory with a GPU device (bubble 69.2 s to 14.0 s).
- Post-processings by kernels: the bubble goes to 5.3 s on the GPU.
- Documentation of the batch path in mfem-mgis; split of the work into
  upstream contributions.

## 12/10/2026: examples, direct solvers, reproducibility

- Examples ex1, ex2, ex3, ex5, ex6 and ex7 by batches and on the GPU.
- Corrections of MFront found by the reviews (static variables, DDIF2,
  Poisson ratio of isotropic DSLs, `rkCastem`).
- hypre on the host with the device on the GPU, for small systems.
- Reuse of the MUMPS analysis, then the cuDSS direct solver: ex8 on the
  GPU goes from 11.1 s to 2.6 s.
- Ordered assembly of the tangent (12 and 13/10): bit for bit
  reproducible results on the GPU.

## 13/10/2026: execution settings, precision plan, CI decisions

- Branches rebased on the latest official masters (mfem-mgis
  `8fac67336e`, MGIS `e8ef4b4`; TFEL base `ff0959ece` of 12/10).
- Execution settings: `--execution cpu|gpu|mixed`, placement of each
  phase and of each material, behaviour libraries for CPU and GPU, in
  all examples and cases.
- Kernels of mfem-mgis compiled for HIP (AMD GPU: compilation checked).
- Measurements of the imposed pressure and of the post-processings; the
  GPU test suite with the ordered assembly.
- Precision plan: decisions D1 to D9 taken, then single precision
  implemented in TFEL, MGIS and mfem-mgis and tested on the CPU; in the
  evening, the examples (21/21) and mm-opera-hpc (6/6) pass in float.
- Continuous integration and site: decisions E1 to E4, and an audit of
  the whole history before making the repository public.

## 14/10/2026: single precision on the GPU

- Float on the CPU: 1.11 times faster than double on the beam, with the
  same iterations. MFront warns when the criterion of a behaviour is
  looser than 1e-5 in float.
- Float on the GPU: the MGIS CUDA tests (51/51, in double and in float)
  and the mfem-mgis suite (270/270) pass. A first measurement gives 1.31
  times, but one computation of the beam out of three diverges.
- Diagnostic of this divergence, traced to FMA contractions in the
  stiffness kernels, and corrected. Float is then 1.37 times faster than
  double on the GPU, within 3.4e-5 of double.
- The test suite of the cases of the prototype runs in float (134 tests),
  without losing any test in double (143/143).
- Drafts of the CPU level of the continuous integration and of this
  website (not yet launched).
