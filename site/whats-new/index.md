# What's new with this prototype

MMM-GPU-Proto is a prototype of the chain MFront (TFEL), MGIS and
mfem-mgis on CPU and on GPU. It is built as patch series against the
official repositories of each library. The pages below summarise what
the prototype changes, what it adds, the design choices behind it, the
defects it found and corrected in the official code, the pitfalls met
on the way, and the main milestones of the work.

The detailed documents, in French, are in the repository: `README.md`
(start here), `docs/` and `JOURNAL.md`.
