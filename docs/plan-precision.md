# Plan: unified float or double precision along the whole chain

Status: plan of 13/10/2026 (task 9b). An independent review (same
day) checked each finding against the sources and corrected or
completed several of them: it is integrated here. The decisions D1 to D9 were
taken on the evening of 13/10, all following the recommendation (D3: (b) and (a)),
as well as the two separate spack environments in float. The implementation
is described at the end (section "Implementation").

## Objective

A single real type for a whole computation, dictated by the installed MFEM
(`mfem::real_t`): hypre, MGIS, the behaviours generated by MFront, mfem-mgis
and the examples follow. A mix (for example behaviours in double and an
MFEM in float) must be refused with a clear message, at configuration
or at loading, and never give wrong results.

Interest: the GeForce cards, including the one of the machine (RTX 5080),
compute in double much more slowly than in single (1/64 according to the
published specifications); the memory bound phases read
fewer bytes (half as many for the values, 1.5 times fewer for a
CSR matrix with 32 bit indices).

Out of scope: mixed precision (behaviours in double and finite elements in
float, or the reverse). The chosen scheme does not prevent it later
(conversion of the arrays of the materials).

## Current state (observed on 13/10/2026)

MFEM and its dependencies:

- `mfem::real_t` is `float` if MFEM is compiled with `MFEM_USE_SINGLE`
  (`config/config.hpp`), `double` otherwise. `config.mk` writes it too, and
  `FindMFEM.cmake` of mfem-mgis already reads all its variables.
- Spack recipe (MFEM 4.7 and later): variant `precision=single|double`,
  which imposes `hypre precision=single`, `petsc~double` and `mumps+float`.
  MFEM checks hypre (`hypre.hpp`) and PETSc (`petsc.hpp`) at compile time,
  and uses `smumps` in single. cuDSS accepts single.
- MFEM refuses in single: SuiteSparse (UMFPACK), AmgX, GSLIB, SUNDIALS,
  SuperLU, STRUMPACK, Ginkgo, SLEPc, libCEED and a few others. This refusal
  only exists in its `makefile` (`PKGS_NO_SINGLE`), the spack recipe has
  no `conflicts`: the failure comes late, during the build of MFEM. The
  spec of the environment mmm-gpu has 8 of these variants (`+libceed +ginkgo
  +sundials +superlu-dist +strumpack +amgx +suite-sparse +gslib`).

MGIS:

- `mgis::real` is `mgis_real`, defined by the macro `MGIS_REAL_TYPE`
  (`MGIS/Config-c.h`), `double` by default. No CMake option, no
  generated header: an application that defined the macro differently from
  the compiled library would have incompatible structures, without any
  error.
- MGIS does not compile in float as it is: the key of `rdt` in
  `BatchDataView.hxx` (16 sources out of 47), `CUDAFieldOperations.hxx` and
  `HIPFieldOperations.hxx`. With `BatchDataView.hxx` fixed, the whole core
  on CPU compiles.
- The C structures of MGIS and of MFront are identical member by member,
  batch structures of the prototype included.
- The parameters of the behaviours (`setParameter`, default values) are in
  `double`, the bounds in `long double`: this is the binary interface of the
  MFront libraries, independent of the type of the arrays.
- Python, Fortran, Julia and FEniCS bindings: nearly 300 hard-coded `double` (or
  `c_double`, `float64`). The C bindings follow `mgis_real`.

MFront and MTest:

- `mfront_gb_real` is hard-coded to `double` (`MFront/GenericBehaviour/Types.h`),
  and so is `mfront_gmp_real` for the material properties. The behaviours are
  templates parameterized by their numeric type, and the code generated by
  the `generic` interface only uses `mfront::gb::real`.
- The `generic-parallel` interface encodes the factor `rdt` on 64 bits
  (`memcpy` of 8 bytes in `GenericParallelBehaviour/BatchData.h`,
  key `toOrderedKey(const double)` in `Integrate.hxx`), and MGIS shares
  this key: in float, the returned factor is wrong, without error (nvcc
  reports it through `-Wstringop-overread`). Changing the `typedef` is therefore
  not enough for this interface.
- The body of the material properties (`generic`, `generic-parallel`) and of the
  models is computed in hard-coded `double`: correct, but without gain on GPU;
  the writing of the outputs of the models probably does not compile in float
  (not tried).
- No symbol indicates the type of a library: a behaviour compiled in
  double loaded by an MGIS in float would read arrays incorrectly.
- MTest computes in double (`MTest/Types.hxx`) and loads a behaviour without
  checking its type: it would read a behaviour in float incorrectly. The test
  `u3si2_swelling` of ex8 loads the behaviour library of mfem-mgis.
- Convergence criteria of the implicit behaviours: 1e-8 by default, but the
  behaviours of the examples set them to 1e-16 (ex7), 1e-14 (NortonCr), 1e-12
  (MonoCristal_UO2), 1e-11 (Mono_UO2_CosH_Jaco3). In float (relative
  precision 1.2e-7), they are out of reach. `numerical_jacobian_epsilon`
  is 0.1 × epsilon.
- `maximal_time_step_scaling_factor` is `DBL_MAX`: converted to float, it
  gives +inf in practice (undefined behaviour according to the standard), without effect
  on the `std::min` where it is used.

mfem-mgis:

- `FindMFEM.cmake` requires SuiteSparse (fatal error without
  `MFEM_USE_SUITESPARSE`): mfem-mgis cannot be configured with an MFEM in
  float.
- `mfem_mgis::real` is `mgis::real`. A mix of MGIS in double and MFEM in
  float already does not compile (`std::span<real>` on `real_t*` in
  `MaterialArrays.cxx`), but without a clear message.
- `ParameterVariant` (`Parameter.hxx`) contains `real`, not `double`: in
  float, `{"Tolerance", 1e-10}` no longer compiles, nor do `is<double>` and
  `get<double>` (about twenty calls).
- AmgX: the option `enable-amgx` of mfem-mgis is independent of MFEM;
  in float, the wrappers (mode `dDDI`) would read floats as
  doubles, without error.
- MPI: `MPI_DOUBLE` on data in `real` in
  `ComputeResultantForceOnBoundary.cxx` (silent overflow) and
  `MeanThermodynamicForces.ixx` (does not compile). Also:
  `PartialQuadratureFunction.cxx` (`double Eval(...) override`, does not
  compile), `numeric_limits<double>::max()` stored in a `real`
  (`PeriodicNonLinearEvolutionProblem.cxx`). `mfem_mgis::mpi_type<real>`
  (`MPI.hxx`) already exists.
- UMFPACK and GSLIB are conditional in the library and its tests,
  but ex2 chooses UMFPACK without a guard, and ex5 is entirely skipped without
  SuiteSparse.
- Hard-coded tolerances: about twenty in the examples and mm-opera-hpc, 46
  test files of mfem-mgis. A loop of mm-opera-hpc
  (`MacroscropicElasticMaterialProperties.cxx`, `while (... > 1e-10)` in
  `real`) could never end in float (not checked).
- Behaviours built outside of `mfem_mgis_add_behaviours_library`: the
  library `BehaviourTest` of the tests of mfem-mgis, the behaviours of the tests
  of MGIS.

Trial without modifying anything: generated code of behaviours compiled with
`mfront_gb_real` in float (copy of `Types.h` placed before the one of TFEL).

- Examples and mm-opera-hpc, 14 behaviours: 9 compile with the same
  warnings as in double (Elasticity, IsotropicLinearElasticity,
  IsotropicLinearHardeningPlasticity, IsotropicLinearThermoElasticity,
  MonoCristal_UO2, SaintVenantKirchhoffElasticity,
  StationaryLinearHeatTransfer, U3SI2_IrradiationCreep,
  U3SI2_SolidSwelling). 5 fail in their own code: a double
  literal with an object in float gives an expression in double, which TFEL
  refuses to assign or copy into an object in float (`2.*mu*...` in
  NortonViscoplasticityWithThreshold of ex7 and NortonCr, `273.15` with
  `auto` in the CoupledHeatTransfer of ex8, a copy initialization
  in Mono_UO2_CosH_Jaco3).
- Tests of mfem-mgis, 17 behaviours: Mazars fails in its code,
  MicromorphicDamageI_SpectralSplit in TFEL itself
  (`StensorComputeIsotropicFunctionDerivative.hxx`, a function that returns
  `0.5`).
- nvcc, `generic-parallel` interface: 3 sources out of 4 compile, the
  fourth (CoupledHeatTransfer) fails as on the CPU.

Extended trial (validation, item 2, done on 13/10/2026): the 856 MFront
files of TFEL (tests, test suite, gallery), of MGIS, of mfem-mgis,
of the examples and of mm-opera-hpc, `generic` interface, on the CPU, with
`mfront_gb_real` in float. 99 do not pass the generation (DSL or options
not handled by the trial), 46 do not compile in double either (old
files): 711 files remain, of which 580 compile in float and 131
fail only in float (18 %). Causes:

- `std::max` and `std::min` on a double and a float: 39 (the deduction
  of the template parameters fails, no conversion at assignment can
  help);
- construction of a tensor from an expression in double: 29;
- assignment or conversion of an expression in double to an object in
  float: about thirty;
- models (outputs in `map<double>`): 9;
- in the headers of TFEL themselves: 18 (criteria of Cazacu, of
  Gurson-Tvergaard-Needleman, of Rousselier, of Mohr-Coulomb, elasticity
  tensor, nucleation, `StensorComputeIsotropicFunctionDerivative`).

By origin: TFEL 97 failures out of 641, MGIS 2 out of 28, mfem-mgis 2 out of 17,
examples 5 out of 19, mm-opera-hpc 3 out of 6. Option D3 (a) alone would
therefore only fix part of the behaviours (assignments and constructions):
the mixed calls to `std::max` and `std::min` and the code of TFEL require
code fixes anyway.

## Proposal

### Source of truth

The type is the one of MFEM (`MFEM_USE_SINGLE` of `config.mk`). There is no
option of mfem-mgis to choose it: changing the precision means
installing another MFEM (spack variant `precision`), which also sets hypre,
PETSc and MUMPS.

### TFEL (MFront, MTest)

- `Types.h` of the behaviours and of the material properties: the type is the macro
  `MFRONT_GB_REAL_TYPE` (`MFRONT_GMP_REAL_TYPE`) if it is defined,
  `double` otherwise. Nothing changes for those who do not define it.
- An option of `mfront` (D2) writes the two macros at the top of the generated
  sources (a library can contain behaviours and properties, like
  `libU3SI2.so` of ex8).
- Each library exports the size of its real type, per function
  (symbol `<function>_real_size`, computed at compile time), written by the
  three generators (`GenericBehaviourSymbolsGenerator`, `generic` and
  `generic-parallel` properties). The API versions (`_api_version`,
  `_generic_parallel_api_version`) also change in float: an old MGIS
  then refuses the library instead of reading it incorrectly. The
  `generic` material properties do not export an API version: an
  old caller (MGIS or MTest from before this work) would read such a
  property in float incorrectly (documented gap).
- `generic-parallel`: the factor `rdt` is encoded and read back converted to
  double, on both sides (MFront and MGIS).
- Code of TFEL itself written for any numeric type
  (`StensorComputeIsotropicFunctionDerivative` and what the extended trial
  will find); double literals in the code of the behaviours: D3.
- Material properties: interface in the chosen type, body in double
  unchanged at first. Models: refused in float at
  loading at first (D9).
- MTest: checks `<function>_real_size` at loading and refuses a behaviour
  that is not in double.

### MGIS

- CMake option `MGIS_REAL_TYPE` (`double` by default, `float`), written
  in an installed header (`MGIS/RealType.h`, included by `Config-c.h`) and
  exported by the installed CMake configuration (like `MGIS_HAVE_TFEL`).
  The macro defined by an application becomes an error if it
  contradicts the header.
- `BatchDataView.hxx` (key of `rdt` through double), `CUDAFieldOperations.hxx`
  and `HIPFieldOperations.hxx` fixed.
- Check at loading in `LibrariesManager::checkBatchInterface`,
  through which the twenty accessors go (behaviours, initializations,
  post-processings, rotations, batch functions, properties): if the
  symbol `<function>_real_size` exists, it must be equal to
  `sizeof(mgis::real)`; otherwise the library is old, hence in
  double, and refused if `mgis::real` is not. Message: name of the behaviour,
  of the two types, and how to rebuild. Only remaining gap: a
  library without the symbol compiled in float by an old `mfront` with
  the macro as a flag, taken for double (rare, documented).
- Parameters and bounds: interface unchanged in double (D6).
- Bindings (Python, Fortran, Julia, FEniCS) in float: refused at
  configuration at first (D5).

### mfem-mgis

- Configuration: `MFEM_USE_SINGLE` of `config.mk`, `MGIS_REAL_TYPE` of the
  configuration of MGIS, clear error if they differ; `static_assert`
  in a source of the library (not in `Config.hxx`, which does not include
  MFEM).
- `FindMFEM.cmake` no longer requires SuiteSparse; what uses it is already
  conditional.
- `enable-amgx` refused with an MFEM in float (D9 can open the mode
  `dFFI` later).
- `ParameterVariant` keeps `double` values (D8); conversion to `real` when
  read.
- MPI through `mfem_mgis::mpi_type<real>` wherever the data are in
  `real`; `PartialQuadratureFunction::Eval` and the
  `numeric_limits<double>` fixed.
- `mfem_mgis_add_behaviours_library` and the library `BehaviourTest`
  pass the type to `mfront` (option of D2); the variable
  `<target>_REAL_TYPE` gives it to the examples.
- Implicit behaviours: `epsilon` and `numerical_jacobian_epsilon` bounded in
  float from the start (D7).
- Relative tolerances of the solvers (D7): a function
  `mfem_mgis::getDefaultRelativeTolerance(t)` that returns `t` in double and
  `max(t, k ε)` in float; the absolute tolerances have a unit and remain
  the responsibility of the caller.

### Examples and mm-opera-hpc

- Relative tolerances through the function above, references compared
  with a tolerance deduced from the type; loop of mm-opera-hpc bounded.
- ex2: UMFPACK only if MFEM has it.
- Behaviours fixed according to D3; the `.mtest` of ex8 reads the library in
  double built for MTest (D4).

## Validation

1. In double: all the suites unchanged (CPU, GPU, examples,
   mm-opera-hpc), same results bit for bit. It is the default path,
   it must not move.
2. Extended compilation trial before implementing D3: all the behaviours of the
   tests of MGIS, of mfem-mgis, of the examples, of mm-opera-hpc and of TDLS,
   in float, on the CPU and with nvcc.
3. In float, on the CPU: separate spack environment (MFEM
   `precision=single`, hypre, MUMPS; without the 8 refused variants, without
   CUDA, short compilation), MGIS in float; suites of mfem-mgis and
   examples, list of the skipped tests and why, refusal of the mixes (behaviour
   in double loaded in float, MGIS and MFEM different, MTest and behaviour in
   float, old MGIS and behaviour in float).
4. In float, on the GPU: separate spack environment with CUDA (long
   compilation of MFEM, see `deps/spack/README.md`), without AmgX.
5. Measurements on the beam: time and number of iterations, float against
   double, on the CPU and on the GPU; gap of the solution; convergence
   of the implicit behaviours with their bounded criteria.

## Decisions to take

- D1. Source of truth: the installed MFEM, no option specific to
  mfem-mgis. Recommended (requested).
- D2. How MFront receives the type. (a) Macros only, passed through the
  compilation flags of each backend. (b) Macros, plus an option
  of `mfront` that writes them in the generated sources. Recommended: (b),
  a single entry point whatever the backend; the size symbol
  protects in both cases.
- D3. Double literals in the code of the behaviours. (a) TFEL accepts
  the assignment and the construction of an object in float from an
  expression of a wider type, with conversion; the existing behaviours
  compile as they are, but some computations stay in double, even on
  the GPU, without any report. (b) Rewrite the behaviours with typed
  literals (`real(2)`), TFEL stays strict. Recommended: (b) for the behaviours of the
  examples and of the tests (honest measurements, warnings
  `-Wdouble-promotion` and of ptxas to find the rest), and (a) in addition
  to accept the existing behaviours of the users. (a) is a
  design change of TFEL, to be proposed to its authors before any
  publication. According to the extended trial, (a) does not cover the mixed calls
  to `std::max` and `std::min` (39 behaviours out of 131): (b) is needed for
  part of the behaviours anyway.
- D4. MTest in float. The CMake function also builds a library
  in double for the MTest tests (ex8), and MTest refuses a behaviour in float.
  Recommended.
- D5. Bindings of MGIS (Python, Fortran, Julia, FEniCS) in float:
  refused at configuration to begin with. Recommended.
- D6. Interface of the parameters of the behaviours (double) and of the bounds (long
  double): unchanged. Recommended: they are input data,
  converted when used, and changing it would break all the existing
  libraries.
- D7. Criteria: in float, `epsilon` of the implicit behaviours bounded at
  `k_l ε` (and `numerical_jacobian_epsilon` in proportion) by
  `setParameter` at loading, with a message; relative tolerances of the
  solvers bounded at `k ε`. `k` and `k_l` to be measured (100 at first).
- D8. Parameters of mfem-mgis (`ParameterVariant`): `double`, and not
  `real`, converted when read. Recommended: the API does not depend on the
  precision, and `{"Tolerance", 1e-10}` still compiles.
- D9. Scope of the first version in float: behaviours only;
  material properties with the interface in float but computed in double;
  models refused; AmgX refused. Recommended.

Before building: agreement on the two separate spack environments in
float (CPU, then GPU). The main environment and its MFEM and AmgX are
not touched.

## Steps

1. Extended compilation trial (validation, item 2): done on the CPU
   (see the current state); remaining: nvcc, and the precise list of the fixes
   of TFEL and of the behaviours.
2. TFEL: macros, option of `mfront`, size symbol, API versions,
   key of `rdt`, code of TFEL for any type, D3, MTest; tests of TFEL
   (a behaviour of each DSL compiled and integrated in float, the `generic-parallel`
   interface with nvcc).
3. MGIS: CMake option, installed header and configuration,
   `BatchDataView.hxx` and operations on the fields, check at
   loading, refusal of the bindings; tests of MGIS in float and in double,
   tests of the refusals.
4. mfem-mgis: configuration, optional SuiteSparse, AmgX refused,
   `ParameterVariant`, MPI, criteria and tolerances, CMake function and
   `BehaviourTest`; double unchanged, float on the CPU.
5. Examples and mm-opera-hpc; then float on the GPU.
6. Measurements, documentation (guide of mfem-mgis, pages of MGIS and of
   MFront).

## Implementation

Done on 13/10/2026:

- TFEL: DSL option `real_type` (`double` by default, or `float`),
  macros `MFRONT_GB_REAL_TYPE` and `MFRONT_GMP_REAL_TYPE` written at the top of the
  sources of the `generic` and `generic-parallel` interfaces, symbol
  `<f>_real_size`, API versions shifted by 1000 in a type other than
  `double`, key of `rdt` built in `double`. The other interfaces and
  the models refuse `float`. MTest refuses a behaviour that is not in
  `double`. `IsAssignableTo` accepts a floating point type to a narrower
  floating point type (D3 a). In double, the generated code only changes by the size
  symbol.
- MGIS: option `MGIS_REAL_TYPE`, generated header `MGIS/RealType.h`, type
  checked at the loading of each entry point, expected versions
  shifted as in MFront. In `float`, the criterion `epsilon` of the implicit
  behaviours was raised to 10 ε (1.2e-6) at loading (first version
  of D7, replaced by a bound of MFront at ε/10, see below). Tests:
  51/51 in double, 47/47 in float.
- Environment `deps/spack/mmm-float-cpu`: MFEM 4.10
  `precision=single`, hypre and MUMPS in single.

What the trials taught:

- The factor of the criterion can be measured: with 100 ε (1.2e-5), a strain
  increment of 5e-5 of the Norton behaviour of the tests is considered converged before
  the first iteration, with a zero stress and a success. 10 ε gives
  the right result on this test; the error on the viscoplastic strain
  then stays bounded by the criterion (2.2e-6 at the start of the creep, then less
  than 1e-9). The polycrystal of mm-opera-hpc then showed that 10 ε is
  still too loose (see below): the chosen bound is ε/10.
- A behaviour written in units where its coefficients leave the range
  of `float` cannot run in `float`: the Norton behaviour of the tests of
  MGIS (`A = 8e-67`, in Pa) gave `A = 0` and an overflow of
  `seq^n`. It was rewritten `A (seq/K)^n` with `A = 1` and
  `K = A^(-1/n)`, which only changes the result in double up to
  rounding. The behaviours of the users will often have to be written in MPa.
- The tolerances of the tests written for double (1e-14) make no
  sense in `float`: they are bounded at 10 ε, or given explicitly
  for `float` when the error depends on the computation.

Continuation (13/10/2026, evening):

- D7 adopted with measured values: the criterion given by `@Epsilon`
  is bounded at ε/10 (1.2e-8) by MFront at generation, with a
  warning, and `numerical_jacobian_epsilon` at ε (1.2e-7), including its
  default value (a tenth of the criterion, raised without warning).
  The default criterion, without `@Epsilon` (1e-8), close to the bound, is
  kept. The warnings written for double ("1e-14 is a good
  choice") are no longer given in float; tests of mfront-query on the
  bounded values (TFEL a84f8012e). A first version bounded at
  loading in MGIS, through `setParameter`: it overwrote the values
  given at run time (parameter file, `setParameter`), hence the
  move into MFront. Only the parameters `epsilon` and
  `numerical_jacobian_epsilon` are bounded: another criterion written for
  double (local criterion of a behaviour, relative bound of a brick) passes
  unchanged.
- Why ε/10 and not 10 ε (first value): the criteria of these DSLs
  are absolute and the residuals are expressed in strains. With
  elastic strains of 1e-4, a criterion of 1.2e-6 leaves about
  1 % of error on the stresses. On the polycrystal of mm-opera-hpc
  (single crystal behaviour in finite strain, `@Epsilon 1e-11`), the
  first Newton iteration of the structure brought the residual down to 0.45
  of its initial value with 10 ε, against 0.024 with 1e-8 or 1e-10, which
  the local solver reaches in float; Newton did not converge. ε/10 is
  the order of the default criterion of the implicit DSL (1e-8), reached by
  residuals in strains up to about 0.1. The perturbation of the
  numerical Jacobian keeps its own bound: a perturbation smaller
  than ε is lost in the rounding of the unknowns. Tests of MGIS in float:
  49/49 with these bounds.
- Relative tolerances of the solvers of mfem-mgis
  (`boundRelativeTolerance`) bounded at 100 ε (1.2e-5): the relative
  residuals of the tests stagnate around 10 ε (1.2e-6), and a bound of 10 ε
  left Newton just above. A zero tolerance (fixed number
  of iterations) and the absolute tolerances are not touched.
- Models: only those of the `Model` DSL are refused in float; the models
  written with a behaviour DSL (`RungeKuttaModelDSL`) are generated in float.
- mfem-mgis: configuration (type of MFEM, refusal of an MGIS of another type
  and of AmgX in float), parameters stored in `double`, MPI types, tests
  compiled in float; double unchanged (216/216 tests on the CPU; the tests
  on the GPU in double were run again at the end, see below). Tests adapted to float
  (`tests/Tolerance.hxx`: tolerance of double bounded at 10 ε, or given
  explicitly for float, from the measured error, with a margin
  of about ten): 204/204 in float on the CPU, with the behaviours regenerated
  by the current mfront. Twelve
  tests remain in double only: ParaSails (NaN from the first
  iteration: hypre in single calls `dpotrf_` and `dpotrs_` of LAPACK in
  double; libHYPRE.so in single imports seven LAPACK routines in double,
  defect of hypre), UMFPACK, GSLIB, Umpire, cuDSS and CUDA, absent
  from the MFEM in float. Defect found in the tests: four MPI reductions
  in `MPI_DOUBLE` on `real` values ("stack smashing" in parallel). The
  mfront sources are regenerated when mfront changes (dependency of the
  commands). `Tolerance.hxx` is installed with ex1 and ex3, whose
  sources are tests.
- Floor of the residual in finite strain: the deformation gradient,
  close to the identity, is only known in float to within one ulp
  (6e-8 below 1, 1.2e-7 above), so the stresses are only
  known to within about 1.5e4 to 3e4 Pa for UO2. The residual of the
  polycrystal and of the cermet of mm-opera-hpc stagnates around 6e3 whatever
  the loading: a tolerance relative to the
  initial residual of a step cannot reach it. mm-opera-hpc (9b31d94)
  takes in float an absolute tolerance of ten times this floor, kept
  when the criterion of the fixed point iterations changes; MPI types of
  `real`, tolerance of the Kröner loop `max(1e-10, 10 ε)` and at most
  1000 iterations, closest row chosen by the comparisons, within
  1e-6 on the time. Double: 6/6, same files bit for bit.
  Float: 6/6, axial stresses within 0.06 % to 0.15 % of double and within
  0.005 % to 0.23 % of Cast3M (with the threshold of 1e4 Pa of the fixed point on
  the lateral stresses, changed on 14/10, see below).
- Examples: 21/21 in float (satoh, which requires SuiteSparse, and the
  PETSc variants are not declared) and 24/24 in double against the
  current sources of mfem-mgis. The tolerances of Newton and of the comparisons
  remain those of double, and are given explicitly for float
  from the measured residuals and errors: the floor of the residual follows
  the scale of the forces (about 1e-5 of it), so that a
  tolerance relative to the initial residual of a step, small at the end of the
  loading or of the coupling iterations, cannot be reached: an
  absolute tolerance at about ten times this floor is needed. ex8
  also generates its behaviours in double for MTest (D4, in its own
  `CMakeLists.txt`).
- MTest also checks the type of the material properties and of the
  `generic` evolutions.
- Second independent review (scratch/plan-precision/relecture-9b-2.md,
  no severe defect, 3 medium, 24 remarks, 5 mutations) addressed:
  thresholds of TwoLayerCube per solver (a modulus wrong by 2 % escaped two
  cases out of five), measures and integrals summed in double in mfem-mgis and
  compared with the volume computed by MFEM (an MPI reduction of the wrong type
  in `computeMeasure` went unnoticed, with measures of 1e37),

- Diagnosis (results/diagnostic-float-gpu-2026-10-14): by default nvcc
  contracts the multiplications and the additions into
  fused multiply-adds (FMA). In the stiffness kernels, in float, these
  contractions made Newton diverge, or converge to less
  accurate solutions, probably through a drift along the soft
  modes of the beam (bending). Yet the tangents by kernels and element by
  element only differ by 2e-7: float works here close
  to its limit (Newton direction known to within 16 to 32 % at step 8).
- Fix in mfem-mgis: in float, the GPU code of the stiffness kernels
  (standard, FBar, Faltus et al.) is compiled without contraction (CMake
  function `mfem_mgis_disable_device_floating_point_contraction`), at the same
  assembly cost. In double, the contractions are kept (assembly
  13 to 16 % slower without them, convergence unchanged). After the fix, 31
  computations out of 31 converge, within 4.2e-5 at most of double;
  reproducer test `beam-gpu-r4` in cases/beam.
- Measurement done again after the fix
  (results/precision-beam-gpu-2026-10-14-0843): float 1.37 times
  faster than double, same iterations, within 3.4e-5 at most of double.

- Polycrystal in float (results/precision-polycrystal-2026-10-14): on
  one step, the axial stress differs from double by 6.1e-3 at 5e-5 of
  strain and by 1.0e-3 at 2e-4; on a larger step (1 s), the behaviour
  fails and the computation stops. Probable cause: F, stored in float close
  to the identity, only moves by steps of one ulp (1e-3 of a strain of
  5e-5, stress quantum of about 1.5e4 Pa). The cancellation of
  `cosh(x) - 1` in the behaviour is not the cause (form with `expm1` tried).
- Tolerance of the fixed point on the lateral stresses of mm-opera-hpc
  (results/cermet-float-2026-10-14), defect found by the CI simulated in
  float: at 1e4 Pa, the first step of the cermet did not converge in five
  computations out of six (stagnation around 1.1e4 Pa, or oscillation between 1.5e4
  and 1.7e4 Pa), depending on the rounding of the whole computation, behaviour included. The step
  cut in two gave a 2.7 % gap to double at 0.2 s, an effect of the time
  step (0.24 to 0.27 % from double at the same steps). Cause: when the two
  lateral stresses are close to S, the correction of the lateral
  components of F, about (1 + ν) (1 - 2 ν) S / E, rounds to zero below
  half the gap between two neighbouring floats (6e-8 just below 1),
  that is 1.54e4 Pa of residual for UO2. The fixed point now takes at
  least twice this resolution, computed from F and the moduli
  (mm-opera-hpc 05358bd): 30,804 Pa here, twice as much in compression,
  negligible in double. Gap to double of 0.10 to 0.16 % at
  0.2 s, of which about 0.07 % is due to the absolute tolerance of the
  microscopic equilibrium in float (measured in double with this tolerance), and of
  0.06 to 0.07 % at 0.4 s; the test checks the same instants in
  both precisions. Double unchanged bit for bit; polycrystal unchanged at
  0.1 and 0.4 s, and the behaviour still fails with a step of 1 s.

- ParaSails in float (mfem-mgis 3ba3b6e729): a defect of hypre 3.2.0,
  also on its master branch and not yet reported. With an external
  LAPACK in single precision, `src/lapack/_hypre_lapack.h` keeps the
  names of the double type (`dpotrf`, `dgetrf`, `dgels`, `dsygv`...),
  whereas `src/blas/_hypre_blas.h` maps the BLAS names to the single
  precision routines. This also breaks the Gaussian elimination 98, 99,
  198 and 199 of BoomerAMG, Schwarz, FSAI, MGR, AIR, GSMG, LOBPCG and
  AME. Two fixes
  compared (`scratch/parasails-float`): a header with the single
  precision names, and the internal LAPACK of hypre. Both converge in 8
  iterations (no convergence in 1000 before), with the same times for the
  `ij` driver of hypre (ParaSails and PCG, 100³ unknowns). Chosen:
  `hypre ~lapack` in the float environments, no patch to maintain. The
  configuration of mfem-mgis warns about a hypre in single precision with
  an external LAPACK, and the ParaSails tests run in float.
- Newton criterion adapted to the precision (mfem-mgis 11fda6426e, then
  9c2dc8c4e5 after an independent review): option
  `StagnationRelativeTolerance` of the Newton solver (10⁴ ε by default,
  1.2e-3 in float, 2.2e-12 in double). Below it, relative to the reference
  residual, a residual which has not decreased by more than 10 % during
  the last two iterations is accepted as converged, and the best iterate of
  the resolution is kept (the residual at the floor is noisy: from 615 to
  7594 on a step of the finite strain test). A slow convergence, even with
  a factor 0.9 per iteration, is not taken for a stagnation: this matters
  since the reference residual is the one of the prediction, if any, much
  larger than the residuals of the resolution. Beam r4 in float with the
  criterion of the case (1e-8): see
  `results/newton-stagnation-beam-2026-10-14` (floor at 1.36e-5 to 2.02e-5
  of the reference residual). In double, the criterion is never triggered
  in the test suites, and the results of mm-opera-hpc are the same bit for
  bit. Float suite of mfem-mgis on the CPU: 223/223.
